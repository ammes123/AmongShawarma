## 🎮 Roles

### 🔴 The Ketchup Sprayer (Classic)
- **Weapon:** Comically large ketchup squirt gun (Super Soaker XL)
- **Kill:** SPLAT → victim freezes → dissolves into pink goo over 10 seconds
- **Sabotage:** "Ketchup Flood" — entire floor turns red, everyone slips at 2x speed
- **Passive:** Leaves ketchup drips behind you. Follow the trail... if you dare.
- **Voice Line:** *"SAUCE IS BOSS."*

### 🟡 The ShawarmaShifter (Trickster)
- **Ability:** Shapeshift into any living crewmate
- **Kill:** Copy someone → walk up to their friend → SPLAT → blame the person you copied
- **Duration:** 20 seconds shifted
- **Passive:** Move 10% faster while shifted. Your kills show the copied player's name.
- **Voice Line:** *"Trust me, I'm [Copied Name]..."*

### 👑 The ShawarmaDisappearerShifter (Final Boss)
- **Abilities:** Shapeshift + Ketchup Squirt + DISAPPEAR (use all at once)
- **Kill:** Go invisible → shapeshift → dual-wield ketchup → victim IMPLODES into a black hole
- **Duration:** 15 seconds invisible, 25 seconds shifted
- **Passive:** Walk through players while invisible. See everyone's outline through walls.
- **Ultimate Combo (Ghost Kill):** Shapeshifted + Invisible + Ketchup = victim **ceases to exist**. No body. No evidence. Just a "?" on the player list.
- **Voice Line:** *"Boo."* (whispered)
